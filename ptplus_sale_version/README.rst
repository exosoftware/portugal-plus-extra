Portugal - Versions for sale orders and quotations
==================================================

This module introduces the following enhancements:

Portuguese quotations and orders require a unique and sequential serial number.
To maintain compliance, revision numbering and logic are managed through a dedicated field, Internal Number, rather than the standard document number.
